Frequently Asked Questions

What are the cafe timings?

Our cafe is open from Monday to Friday, between 08:00 AM and 04:00 PM for all campus students and faculty members.

Do you deliver outside the campus?

No, our delivery and pickup services are strictly restricted within the Capital University of Science and Technology campus boundaries.

How can I cancel my order?

You can cancel your order within 5 minutes of placing it by contacting our support team directly through the contact page.
