About CUST Cafe

Welcome to CUST Cafe, the ultimate hangout spot located right inside the Capital University of Science and Technology campus! We serve our students and faculty with the fresh taste of premium coffee, crispy burgers, and mouth-watering cheesy snacks at affordable, student-friendly prices.

🚀 Our Future Vision: Smart AI Consultant

We are planning to upgrade CUST Cafe by introducing an advanced **AI Chatbot Consultant** to take care of our customers' health and fitness goals!

If a student or faculty member is feeling unwell (e.g., stomach upset, flu, or allergy), they can simply chat with our AI. The bot will automatically analyze their health condition and recommend safe, light meals like green tea or plain sandwiches while warning them against spicy food.

Additionally, for fitness enthusiasts, the AI will act as a personal diet planner—calculating daily calorie needs and customizing orders to perfectly match their low-carb or high-protein diet charts. This ensures maximum trust and care for our campus community!
